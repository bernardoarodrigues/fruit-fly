# Inhibitory factorial: physiology and promotion gates

These are operational promotion requirements, with subsequent evidence updates. Following the latest user steering, the inhibitory factorial followed independent review of the existing Eon neural results; the body integration benchmark was cancelled. The [executed factorial](inhibitory-factorial.md) passes its numerical checks but retains the same two-cell spike trains in every arm. This qualifies the factors for further comparison, not physiological validation or replacement of the recurrent runtime.

**H1 is an arm name, not a claim of biological validation.** In the [factorial design](inhibitory-factorial-design-review.md), it combines inhibition-only voltage dependence with the always-decay/receive/retain synapse-handling package. Positive input remains current-like. H0 uses the same inhibitory equation with original freeze/reject/reset handling; C0/C1 provide the corresponding current-like controls. Preserve both comparisons, H0−C0 and H1−C1, and their interaction. H1−C0 alone cannot identify which change caused an outcome.

C0 must first reproduce the retained baseline states, spikes and event bookkeeping bitwise. The altered arms must recompute their own threshold and delivery eligibility while retaining the original ordered source events; all mismatches and pending deliveries remain evidence.

## Measured targets that can be used

The [retained Wilson–Laurent 2005 evidence](../research/13-antennal-lobe-inhibitory-constraints.md) supplies response-amplitude fractions after GABA application: picrotoxin blocks **98 ± 3% in LNs (n=6)** and **46 ± 5% in PNs (n=14)**; CGP54626 blocks **97 ± 2% of the remaining PN response (n=5)**, while LN response amplitude is **101 ± 6% of control (n=5)**. Errors are SEM. These can constrain a separately declared receptor-component assay. They are not weight fractions, unitary currents, or an acceptance interval obtained by treating ±SEM as biological tolerance. The present single-component H arms do not explicitly model these drug conditions, so they cannot claim this match without an observation/mechanism definition. The published early/late odor windows, 0–500 ms and 1.5–2.5 s, are not receptor time constants.

The closest retained **inhibitory temporal-transfer** target is [Nagel–Wilson 2016 Figure 6C–F](ln-inhibitory-transfer-source.md): separately measured ChR-positive population firing falls while ChR-negative LN outward current builds. Current is mean ± SEM across 9 cells, with 6 driver-absent controls; firing is from 5 other cells. The separately frozen [phase-2 amendment](../validation/ln-inhibitory-transfer-phase2-plan.json) preserves baseline `[−0.15,−0.05) s`, early `[0.05,0.15) s`, and late `[0.25,0.35) s`, relative to the illustrated light command. Its [extracted graphical enclosures](ln-inhibitory-transfer-phase2.md) resolve late-minus-early outward current at **+1.489 to +2.292 pA** and firing-rate change at **−15.657 to −8.608 spikes/s**. F early/late minus baseline are **+2.876 to +3.740 / +4.745 to +5.637 pA**. These ranges describe mean ink, anchor uncertainty and bin enclosure; they are not SEM or confidence intervals. D/F repeat one current cohort, and their graphical checks agree without averaging. E's SEM and the overlapped control mean remain unavailable. No physiological model comparison was performed. Reported 100 ms acausal PSTH smoothing limits onset/latency interpretation.

For a subsequent compatible population assay, normalized response shape or relative amplitude could be compared after a fixed, explicitly stated within-signal normalization. Do not divide mean current by another cohort's mean firing to create per-spike gain. Exact Figure 6 holding voltage, local chloride/reversal and target identities are unresolved; the model's effective mV state and dimensionless `h` cannot be converted to measured pA or nS from these sources. An absolute-amplitude gate must therefore remain **unresolved**, rather than pass by rescaling the output. This does not prevent the numerical factorial or a clearly labeled relative-response comparison.

The [recovered reversal supplement](wilson-gaba-reversal-source.md) has solution-dependent somatic responses and censored endpoints; its −75 mV holding limitation does not validate H1's reversal. The [Salman workbook](salman-current-clamp-data.md) has a factor-of-two published current-axis inconsistency and incomplete specimen correspondence, so it cannot supply the missing resistance/current conversion. [ORN→PN amplitudes and depression/recovery](orn-pn-physiology-lead.md) are excitatory evidence and must remain separate from inhibitory calibration. Broad female LN/PN populations do not identify the receptors or kinetics of male targets 67052 and 13314; transmitter annotations describe outgoing sign, not incoming receptors.

## Finite recurrent test before promotion

The proposed initial recurrent panel can reuse the [Or42a engineering stimulus contrast](or42a-summary-experiment.md): the same 36 VM7d input identities, no imposed input, constant 11 Hz, a 500 ms 11→149→11 Hz ethyl-acetate profile, a 11→57.679→11 Hz isoamyl-acetate profile, and the ethyl-acetate source-output block. These are imposed excitation-event rates, not afferent spike clamps or concentration calibration; the isoamyl-acetate condition is a reserved chemical lookup, not a held-out prediction. Seeds 11/12/13 provide paired external streams across arms. Actual source and recurrent spikes must be allowed to differ. The 1.5 s source protocol alone is not a persistent-activity assay: freeze a finite extension with baseline recovery and then external input off, keeping these two conditions distinct.

Before running, freeze the comparison arms, duration, populations, amplitude/timing summaries and acceptance tolerances in one plan. For each seed retain voltage distributions and lower-tail fractions, pre-threshold extremes, firing-rate distributions, active-cell fractions, source versus nonsource counts, selected downstream/motor responses, and baseline/post-stimulus trajectories. Set numerical limits separately from engineering activity limits and measured physiological targets. Assess recovery against the matching baseline and no-input control; do not require universal zero spontaneous activity. Report residual growth, saturation or persistent recruitment over the entire declared post-stimulus window. A finite pass supports only that horizon.

Promotion requires the joint result: numerical accuracy and bound, stated physiological agreement at the level actually supported by the data, preserved stimulus contrasts across the declared seeds, and recurrent improvement without unacceptable persistence or collapse. A failed or unresolved component remains visible. Do not retune gains, remove sources, change input amplitudes or redefine success after seeing the result. Exact-target absolute-current calibration remains a limit on the biological claim, not an open-ended prerequisite for completing the bounded engineering comparison.

This note originally defined prospective gates. The later evidence updates link the completed conditional replay and one bounded graphical extraction, including the preserved overlay-frame error and correction. No fitted inhibitory parameters, recurrent result or runtime promotion is claimed.
