# Prospective PN→KC/APL calibration

Status: proposed next scope only. This document launches no inventory, fitting, simulation or intervention. The first step is a bounded read-only audit of existing graph and saved activity.

The [completed comparison](../validation/navigation-mbon-intervention-analysis-results.json) supports investigating PN→KC/APL before changing downstream gains. KC suppression silences MBON12/14 after their transient; MBON13 remains at 252–290 Hz during washout (258–296 Hz during the pulse). Odor contrast stays negligible, restoration returns the ceiling, and global persistence remains. This establishes a model pathway contribution, not a physiological repair or unique upstream cause; KC calibration alone may not fix MBON13.

1. **Inventory before choosing a mechanism.** Freeze identities for all annotated KCs, their subclasses, the available APL cells and upstream PNs, including hemisphere and missing/ambiguous labels. Preserve the previously measured **3,957 KC→MBON12–14 presynaptic cells as a separate subset**, rather than using them as the denominator for all KCs. Use existing controls/branches to compare baseline, pulse, washout and input-off activity and odor contrasts. Join PN→KC, KC→KC and reciprocal KC/APL contacts with transmitter labels, model weights and actual emissions. Separate contacts, nominal delayed-input inventories and available state evidence. Contacts do not enumerate independent claws; missing compartments stay unknown, and input totals do not reconstruct KC voltage. Stop here before choosing one local mechanism comparison.

2. **Match the measurement before defining the objective.** The [retained physiology review](../research/20-kc-mbon-functional-calibration.md) supports sparse, selective KC responses and local APL feedback. Honegger’s approximately 5% calcium recruitment uses baseline significance and repeatability; it is not an any-spike criterion or a universal target. Report simulated any-spike fraction, baseline-subtracted recruitment, spike count and duration separately. A quantitative calcium criterion needs a supported observation model and the source’s trial rule; prefer compatible electrophysiology for spike/amplitude/timing objectives. Keep sex, preparation, subclass, duration and cohort differences explicit. The existing imposed Or42a/VM7d “EA” condition is one sensory channel, not the broad ensemble evoked by 1% ethyl acetate; no gain may be fitted to erase that mismatch.

3. **Freeze one local calibration, preserving its control.** After the inventory, select a supported PN→KC electrophysiological assay and a small declared parameter set. Constrain intrinsic KC response and PN input integration before fitting population feedback, so KC threshold and APL strength cannot compensate freely for each other. Test local APL effects only to the spatial resolution actually supported by the graph and assay. Retain current equations/weights as a control. Use a separate local testbed with fixed PN spike histories, explicit ensembles and baseline/pulse/recovery intervals. Imposed PN replay is not a recurrent sensory circuit. Exclude global gain sweeps, arbitrary 5% constraints, motor adjustments and default changes.

4. **Require numerical and input accounting first.** Record exact source IDs, ordered emissions, delayed arrivals, accepted events, masks, initial state and intervention scope. For stochastic comparisons retain candidate/applied events, actual source spikes and shared RNG realizations; commanded Hz is not emitted Hz. Check zero-input decay, event ordering, available analytic limits and fixed-step convergence. Use independent quadrature/ODE and order-64 checks for the largest conductance and smallest threshold margins under the existing declared numerical tolerances. Preserve threshold ambiguity and failed cases; finite or bounded voltage alone is insufficient.

5. **Evaluate fixed parameters beyond development.** Designate untouched cells, stimulus levels or temporal patterns before fitting for genuinely held-out evaluation. All existing seed 11/12/13 histories and outcomes have already been inspected. Reusing 11 for development and 12/13 for fixed-parameter computational robustness uses previously seen histories, not new held-out data or independent animals. Evaluate spontaneous/evoked amplitudes, spike counts/selectivity, timing, recovery and population overlap. Define tolerances from compatible source uncertainty/resolution before fitting; label engineering tolerances. Require stimulus contrast and recovery, not just fewer spikes. Only afterward consider a separately frozen recurrent full-graph test with the unchanged decoder; local replay success does not establish navigation.

The [negative ORN→PN depression result](orn-pn-depression-shape-boundary.md) remains a constraint: the conditional female-VM2 Abbott fit does not explain the fixed 15/50 Hz evaluation curves, and its inferred recovery depends on unestablished conditioning history and normalization. Do not install that fit as male-VM7d physiology, hide its mismatch in a PN→KC gain, or use a depression filter simply to make KC recruitment look sparse.
