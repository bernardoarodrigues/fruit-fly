## Concrete extraction protocol to freeze

1. **Retain and attribute the artwork.** Save native paths, fill rules, curve controls, transforms, clipping and object IDs for the listed candidates before deriving values. Preserve curve geometry; do not silently replace Bézier curves with straight segments. Render isolated objects and their union over the original panel to confirm identity and continuity. F's black object and E's green union must be separable from axes, labels and other data. Hidden paths beneath raster overlays are not automatically equivalent to the visible published mean.

2. **Calibrate axes with uncertainty.** Use F's own 200 ms and 4 pA bars; use E's labeled rate ticks, including intermediate ticks as checks. Use the shared expanded light-command artwork for time zero. Calibrate D independently with its 1 s and 4 pA bars. Retain finite ink extents of anchor endpoints, tick thickness and calibration residuals. A chosen nominal midpoint of a scale anchor is a coordinate convention, not an inferred neural centerline. Propagate the admissible anchor ranges through the summaries; do not claim more precision from decimal PDF coordinates. The command is not a measured photon or presynaptic spike clock.

3. **Enclose each plotted mean without inventing it.** For each 1 ms-wide display-time bin, compute the full vertical extent of the attributable mean ink over that bin and all admitted calibration positions. These bins are integration partitions, not recovered acquisition samples. Preserve disconnected components and gaps. The enclosing interval represents possible plotted location, not biological variability or a guaranteed error bound on the original recording. Do not take its midpoint as recovered mean data. A union across E's object seams is acceptable only if the render confirms one continuous line. No smoothing, deconvolution, curve fitting or missing-segment interpolation is permitted. Preserve the complete displayed artwork, not only the selected windows.

4. **Use a bounded fallback.** If vector rendering cannot reproduce the visible attributable line, inspect fixed panel crops at 600 and 1200 dpi. Retain color/antialias attribution choices and include boundary pixels conservatively. Keep both resolutions as a graphical sensitivity check; disagreement widens the reported range or makes the quantity unresolved. Do not repeatedly increase resolution, tune segmentation to the expected direction, or infer a control mean through overlapping artwork. Failure to identify one optional control/SEM does not justify inventing it or prevent reporting independently recoverable mean artwork.

5. **Summarize exactly the existing windows.** Baseline is `[−0.15,−0.05) s`, early `[0.05,0.15) s`, and late `[0.25,0.35) s`. Integrate each bin's lower/upper enclosure with its actual overlap duration to enclose the displayed window mean. Use physical current scale in pA and E's plotted spikes/s scale; the current vertical origin is arbitrary until baseline subtraction. Every point in a required window must have attributable support under the admitted calibration. Missing support yields an unavailable window, not an extrapolated value.

If window-mean enclosures are `[L_W,U_W]`, a conservative graphical interval for the late-minus-early difference is `[L_late−U_early, U_late−L_early]`. Report baseline-to-early and baseline-to-late changes separately. Baseline cancels from late-minus-early; do not subtract or count its uncertainty twice. Keep shared calibration effects shared when enumerating alternatives; the enclosing subtraction above may remain conservative. Do not convert these graphical ranges into SEM, confidence intervals, p-values or a likelihood.

## Deliverables and decision rules

One bounded extraction should produce an object/calibration receipt, a compact window/difference table, and a verification overlay. Retain complete source geometry and optional raster crops under ignored raw artifacts; small tracked records identify their hashes. For each quantity expose the cohort, representation, units, exact window, nominal coordinate convention, graphical interval, coverage, missingness and original SEM status. No duplicate biological observations or fabricated centerline arrays are required.

The preregistered descriptive target is **positive late-minus-early outward current and negative late-minus-early rate**. A direction is graphically resolved only if its entire enclosure is strictly on the relevant side of zero. If an enclosure includes zero, report unresolved at graphical precision; retain an opposite direction as a negative result. These tests assess displayed means, not biological statistical significance. Compare independently calibrated D/F summaries without averaging them. A discrepancy outside their graphical ranges is a representation inconsistency to retain, not another replicate or a reason to move the windows.

The resulting current changes can be expressed in **pA relative to baseline even though absolute holding current is absent**. They cannot identify conductance, contact gain or unitary release: Figure 6 holding voltage, relevant reversal and exact target identity are unknown. D/F and E are separate cohorts, so their ratio is not per-spike gain. The 100 ms acausal PSTH smoothing and unspecified current preprocessing preclude extracting a precise causal latency or inhibitory time constant from their offset. The windows describe the displayed onset sequence only; they do not independently measure the full approximately 2 s response or a recovery constant.

This is a population LN-to-LN assay in female flies aged 1-3 days, using broad NP3056 expression and unidentified nonexpressing LNs. It does not identify the receptors or physiology of the male replay targets or the Patchy/Full/Keystone source types. A future relative-shape model comparison must declare its observation mapping separately; this extraction must not normalize model output to conceal absent absolute-current calibration.

Phase 2 remains unperformed. The proposal needs one frozen amendment and one bounded attribution/extraction attempt; no additional paper acquisition, model fit or neural/body simulation is part of it.
