# First active C0 baseline: independent saved-data review

The completed C0, seed 11, constant-baseline trial passes **38,229/38,229 independent checks on the first audit execution**. The [receipt](../validation/inhibitory-recurrent-panel-01-C0-seed11-constant_baseline-active-review.json) records no failures. The new [active reader](../scripts/review_inhibitory_recurrent_panel_active.py) imports only the earlier independent archive decoder and ordinary Python/NumPy tools; it imports no producer, neural kernel or metric helper. No network was rerun.

All 600 chunks / 30,000 ticks were read, covering 611 archives and 19,319 arrays. The raw stream contains **2,332,500 spikes**, with 618 external event candidates and 617 applied direct events. The rejected candidate agrees with the actual source firing-tick mask. Shared RNG boundaries, the constant 11 Hz input for 1.5 seconds and zero-rate final 1.5 seconds match the frozen plan.

Every stored transition of all 48 selected cells was checked. Their availability and firing masks follow their own saved spike histories. The original signed-current interval equation reconstructs their prethreshold voltage byte for byte; direct 68.75 mV increments and resets reproduce each following voltage boundary exactly. Independent delayed incoming-edge reconstruction and the declared synaptic decay/reset rules reproduce their selected synaptic state bytes. The 344,181 logged selected-edge records match the independently reconstructed tick, source, original CSR edge index, target and disposition in order. The full-graph delivery-visit count follows the raw delayed source stream; accepted/unavailable global totals are checked as a partition, while their exact dispositions are reconstructed for selected targets.

All complete checkpoints agree with raw-spike last-firing histories, valid pending queues, selected boundary states and independently accumulated per-cell counts. The durable C0 prefix gate is verified against its saved checkpoint marker and original trace. The first 1.5 seconds reproduce the old ordered spikes, actual input masks, RNG boundaries, full endpoint voltage/synaptic arrays and all-cell window counts.

The final three half-second bins contain 438,307, 440,585 and 437,192 nonsource spikes, respectively. Source spikes are zero in all three bins. These correspond to about 5.260, 5.287 and 5.246 Hz per nonsource cell, so nonsource activity persists across the entire retained input-off interval. The fixed forward motor pair emits no spikes in any of the seven windows. All count/cohort summaries were reconstructed without the producer's metric helper. These are observations of this one engineering control, not physiological validation, a long-term stability result or an H1 comparison.
