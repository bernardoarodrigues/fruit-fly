# Graded electrical state and conditional active recovery

Completed 2026-09-06 UTC. This step implements an executable experimental state model and compares a slow outward-current candidate with passive-only recovery across all eleven dedicated 2 nA cells. It follows the [positive passive modes](42-apl-positive-passive-modes.md). The candidate improves conditional transfer for four of five evaluation flies, while leaving a clear gap: the outward-current state is initialized at pulse offset rather than generated by a validated stimulation/calcium rule.

## Executable state model

[fruitfly/graded_apl.py](../fruitfly/graded_apl.py) defines passive voltage-mode states x_j and a nonnegative outward-current state a, with units seconds, mV, pA and MΩ:

`dx_j/dt = (-x_j + R_j * (I - a) / 1000) / tau_j`

`da/dt = (a_target - a) / tau_a`

The outward target is an explicit caller input. The implementation does not infer it from spikes, voltage, calcium or synaptic activity. Absolute resting voltage is an external offset. The existing neural runtime does not import or enable this module.

The constant-input update integrates both states exactly, including the coupling from exponentially changing outward current to each voltage mode. The convolution uses a numerically stable expression for equal or nearly equal passive/outward time constants. Four focused tests pass: comparison with an independent ODE integrator, timestep subdivision, equal/nearly equal time constants, agreement between vector recovery and state updates, and zero-input long-time relaxation. This verifies the declared linear state equations, not physiological sufficiency. A current-based outward state alone does not impose an ionic reversal-potential voltage bound; that requirement remains before recurrent promotion.

## Frozen complete comparison

The [plan](../validation/apl-active-recovery-plan.json), SHA-256 `e3a6fb9760db8e3b8b5b75e08fe849dca35cc60158323faf6eb967a38d9709c1`, matches all eleven AHP cells to their passive recordings. All four passive fitting-window variants are carried forward with their resistance and time constants fixed.

Six fly IDs are assigned to calibration, three controls and three RNAi, by a declared hash ranking within genotype. The remaining five flies (three controls, two RNAi) are excluded from parameter fitting. These are disjoint source fly IDs; repeated sweeps remain within each cell. Every waveform was previously inspected, so this is **retrospective conditional transfer**, not a blind test.

For each cell, the voltage deflection immediately before current offset is measured over the final 1 ms relative to the source baseline. That initial deflection is distributed among passive modes according to their modeled contributions to a 750 ms pulse. This modal allocation is an assumption. Initial conditions and passive parameters use measurements from each evaluation cell; this is not a prediction of the preceding 2 nA depolarization.

After offset, injected current and outward target are zero. A shared outward decay time and two nonnegative initial-current amplitudes (one for control, one for RNAi) are fit to the six calibration mean waveforms over **50–800 ms**. The common tau is bounded at 50 ms–3 s. Amplitudes are solved by nonnegative scalar least squares at each tau; a fixed log grid and local refinement retain the boundary candidates. All four passive variants are fit separately, with no selection by evaluation error.

The observed and predicted waveforms use identical nonoverlapping averages of ten consecutive 10 kHz samples into 1 ms bins. This declared observation rule reduces the cost of full-waveform comparison; it is not a change to the earlier threshold-crossing measurements or an attempt to smooth away their ambiguity. Cells receive equal fitting weight, rather than weight proportional to their number of recorded sweeps. All individual observed sweeps are retained in the [saved arrays](../validation/apl-active-recovery-arrays.npz).

RMSE is scored over 50–2,800 ms. The full [results](../validation/apl-active-recovery-results.json) also preserve early and 800–2,800 ms late-window scores for every cell/variant. No fitted outward tau reaches its bound.

For every passive variant, active recovery lowers full-window error in **four of five evaluation flies** and **five of six calibration flies**. The weak-response evaluation RNAi cell `250522f01c01R` is not improved: its observed mean has little consistent early AHP, and the group-shared active amplitude overpredicts that response. This failure is retained. No cell-specific amplitude is added to make it disappear. Calibration improvement alone is not claimed as independent validation.

The estimated outward amplitudes are phenomenological parameters of this conditional current model. Their difference is consistent with the source's RNAi comparison, but they are not measured SK conductances, independently identified calcium levels, male-specific parameters or a natural synaptic drive law. Across passive variants, similar output waveforms coexist with parameter shifts; the four variants remain an uncertainty set rather than a globally selected parameter vector.

## Independent verification and visualization

The [independent reviewer](../scripts/review_apl_active_recovery.py) constructs the continuous three-state matrix, takes its matrix exponential for a 0.1 ms timestep, and runs a separate discrete state simulation. All **88 passive/active predicted traces** (eleven cells × four variants × two models) agree with the analytic convolution route. Maximum difference is **3.36 × 10⁻¹³ mV**. All **264** early/full/late RMSE calculations and the four calibration objectives are reproduced, and calibration/evaluation fly disjointness is checked. The optimizer itself is not independently repeated in this review; the objective and predictions are verified.

The [complete figure](../validation/apl-active-recovery.png) was rendered and visually inspected. Every cell is labeled with genotype and partition, all observed sweeps and four active variants are shown, and all evaluation comparisons appear in the final panel. Several traces retain baseline drift, variable trough timing or residual late hyperpolarization that this three-parameter active fit does not capture.

## Next step and promotion boundary

This supplies an executable, timestep-stable local candidate and a positive conditional recovery result. It does **not** yet make the APL response autonomous under arbitrary input. Next measure the complete depolarizing current/voltage response and stimulus history in the available firing-pattern and dedicated-pulse recordings. Use those constraints to define how an outward-current or conductance state is recruited during stimulation, then test the full pulse and recovery together without prescribing the post-pulse state from the answer.

Keep the weak-cell transfer failure, passive variant uncertainty and fly-level grouping visible. Before a recurrent network experiment, address conductance/reversal bounds, natural KC→APL input scaling, local GABA release mapping and the explicit mated-female ex vivo transfer to the male graph. Those are separate from numerical state-update correctness. H1 remains unpromoted; no global E/I gain sweep, body/decoder change or cancelled Eon integration occurred.
