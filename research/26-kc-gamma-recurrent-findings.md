# γ recurrence, residual excitation and output saturation

The completed [six-case diagnostic](../docs/kc-gamma-intervention-results.md) supports three distinct conclusions about the current model. It does not identify a physiological replacement.

1. **The fast positive γ-lobe term contributes materially to excessive γ firing.** Removing only its allocated contact contribution cuts pulse rates by 54.17–54.53% and increases the paired narrow-odor contrast in all three seeds. This localizes a model dependence to a specific source/target/ROI term. It does not establish that those anatomical contacts have no electrical effect or that a zero term is an appropriate receptor model.
2. **Residual high activity has other supports.** The γ population still averages about 155 Hz during the pulse, other KCs stay near 294 Hz, and model APL/MBONs remain at the exact refractory ceiling. This rules out the selected γ fast term as a sufficient repair. It does not identify which residual pathways or intrinsic parameters are correct or incorrect. The prior KC input inventory implicates multiple candidate model assumptions; further causal or physiological constraints are required to separate them.
3. **Saturated firing obscures substantial state changes.** APL/MBON p-state decreases at the matched 1 s checkpoint while coarse rates remain unchanged. Lack of a firing-rate effect cannot be used here as evidence that upstream input was unaffected. Conversely, observed γ voltages become more positive while γ firing decreases, accompanied by fewer sampled cells at reset. Voltage appearance alone would rank these changes ambiguously.

The small γ contrast improvement does not propagate cleanly through the retained navigation readouts. MBON pulse contrasts remain zero; FB5AB contrasts vary in sign across intervention seeds. These are model observations, not a biological claim that the corresponding circuit fails to carry odor value.

After original delivery returns and input is removed together, the γ/all-KC and global high-activity state returns. This is evidence of failure of this *temporary diagnostic* to remove the persistent phenotype. It is not evidence about the behavior of a permanently installed, properly calibrated metabotropic mechanism, because neither its kinetics nor its release/voltage relationship was simulated.

Potential next knowledge comes from matching observables before fitting: the public adult γ-KC data use calcium, acetylcholine and cAMP reporters, whereas the current recurrent diagnostic records point-cell voltage, p/h and spikes. Raw time axes, perturbation protocols, population identity, reporter filtering and sex/age coverage must be inspected. The source review's public XLSX and author-code leads remain uninspected at this milestone. APL likewise needs a justified graded release/readout mapping. Neither missing mapping should be filled by arbitrary gains chosen to make walking occur.

All results belong to the same six historical seeds/conditions. The independent raw-count, scalar-reference and whole-trial sorting calculations were performed by one agent; they strengthen implementation evidence but are not independent biological replication or a separate-person review. H1 remains experimental, the Eon embodied benchmark remains cancelled, and no runtime/body/decoder default changed.
