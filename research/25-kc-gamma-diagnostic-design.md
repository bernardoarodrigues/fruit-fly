# From contact location to a bounded recurrent test

The prepared [γ-KC diagnostic](../docs/kc-gamma-intervention-plan.md) turns the spatial/receptor audit into a causal model test. It preserves the control and makes no fitted physiological claim.

The original spatial count includes 451,534 KC→KC contacts in literal gL ROIs. Requiring an annotated γ postsynaptic KC selects 414,095; 37,439 lie on other KC target types. **Derived insight:** a neuropil-only mask would change additional cell populations. ROI and cell type should be intersected, rather than treated as interchangeable anatomical descriptions. This difference is an exact dataset count, not evidence that any label or connection is erroneous.

Likewise, 28,506 selected neuron pairs also contain unselected contacts. Suppressing every pair with any γ-lobe contact would remove another 46,992 contacts' allocated contribution. The contact-aware calculation avoids that extra intervention. Allocation still inherits the model's equal-contact assumption and the rounding of its original float32 pair weight.

The intervention affects all 1,557 annotated γ KCs but samples continuous traces in only thirteen anatomical representatives. Full population spike histories and checkpoints support population measures; representative voltage plots cannot establish all-cell physiology. α′β′ and αβ sources contribute a small minority of selected contacts; source subtype counts are retained in the mask receipt, not silently excluded after observing results.

The design deliberately restores the original term at the same time external input ends, matching the earlier H1 histories. **Interpretive limit:** a resulting rebound can be associated with this joint transition, but cannot isolate restoration from withdrawal without a separately declared future control. Divergent recurrent trajectories are expected and must be measured, not forced back to a common history.

Validation before launch includes 355 manufactured kernel checks, six inactive checkpoint probes with 344 checks and exact historical output parity, and a twelve-cell end-to-end worker fixture with 5,016 online worker checks plus 1,528 archive/publication checks. The independent mask reconstruction uses a separate raw-row/counting method, but the implementation and review in this turn were performed by one agent; no separate reviewer is claimed. These checks demonstrate mechanics and accounting, not biological fidelity.

One development import failed before any network advanced: a source replacement suffix also matched the reference-merging call. The guarded derivation refused ambiguity. The failed source and receipt are retained under `validation/kc-gamma-intervention-development/`; the anchor was narrowed before the successful checks or scientific plan. No scientific result has been retried or replaced.

Test all three matched stimulus contrasts and off-period dynamics together. No local improvement promotes H1, installs a receptor law, or makes the physical fly navigate.
