# Adult obstacle avoidance: bounded evidence review

Research snapshot 2026-09-05. This review was paused to prioritize the user's requested Eon integration audit. Findings below are source-backed, but full methods and quantitative sensory transfer remain incomplete. No runtime changes, neural simulations or steering rules were introduced.

The strongest current tactile candidate is **TwoLumps Ascending (TLA) → Moonwalker Descending Neurons (MDNs)**. The retained MaleCNS annotations explicitly name the TLA correspondence. Existing JO-F work establishes an imposed grooming assay, not a wall-avoidance transducer; the club proprioception proxy also cannot stand in for bristle deflection or contact force.

| Evidence | Measured stimulus, response and scope | Exact male join / unresolved boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Sen, Wang & Dickson 2019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2019.11.004) | TLA silencing reduces retreat at physical barriers in darkness; optogenetic activation induces backward walking, partly mediated by excitatory recruitment of MDNs. Calcium responses follow activation of NOMPC-expressing neurons. | `AN17A026`: 13845, 14010, explicitly annotated as TLA. Four exact `MDN` cells: 10763, 11288, 11332, 12348. The experimental NOMPC sensory population is not an exact receptor crosswalk; full methods/sex not yet retrieved. |
| [Liessem et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2026.06.045) | Inward antennal deflection evokes backward stepping with turning. MDN firing rises within 100 ms and remains elevated for roughly 500 ms. Unilateral touch recruits MDNs bilaterally despite lateralized turning. Published mechanical protocol: ten 2-s pokes, 10-s interstimulus interval. | Exact MDNs above; no exact peripheral antennal receptor population established. Thus contact side cannot simply be translated into asymmetric MDN drive. Indexed publisher methods were available; direct full-text retrieval returned 403. |
| [Medeiros et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2024.05.021) | Leg mechanosensory-bristle activation elicits directional avoidance and sustained motor activity even without the central brain, with an initial poorly coordinated phase. | This is a VNC route, not evidence that all tactile signals require a DN. No exact experimental driver-to-MaleCNS bristle-cell mapping obtained. Institutional PDF returned 403; abstract-level claim ceiling retained. |
| [Sen et al. 2017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2017.02.008), [Wu et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.21022) | LC16 activation/visual threat supports backward walking and turning via MDN-related pathways. Wu's LC16-silencing experiment did not establish necessity for looming retreat, consistent with alternative pathways. | Exact `LC16`: 88 left/94 right by soma side. Visual projection cells are not eye pixels or tactile afferents. A retinal transduction and intermediate-circuit audit remains required. |
| [Yang et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2024.08.033) | DNa02/DNg13 activity and perturbations support distinct leg gestures during steering: inside-stride attenuation and outside-stride extension, with gait-phase dependence. | `DNa02`: 10360, 523769; `DNg13`: 11074, 512006. This is a motor-output constraint, not a demonstrated wall-input pathway or permission to add a turn heuristic. |
| [Ache et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2019.01.079) | LPLC2/LC4 provide looming size/speed information to the giant-fiber escape pathway. | Exact `LPLC2`, `LC4`, `DNp01` populations are retained. This is a takeoff-related comparator, not a walking-avoidance replacement. |

The [identity receipt](../validation/obstacle-avoidance-identities.json) stores all candidate IDs, graph indices, annotation fields and all eight possible TLA→MDN pairs. Seven retained pairs contain **255 contacts**; one has zero retained contact. Both TLA cells are predicted cholinergic in this imported model. These are anatomical/model facts, not measured synaptic efficacy or reproduced avoidance. The receipt also preserves direct mechanosensory inputs without promoting them into a stimulus group. `SNta33` examples enter through MesoLN, illustrating why a coarse leg label is insufficient to assign an anterior-wall receptor. Broad `leg bristle` labels must not be used alone: anatomical bristles can carry other sensory modalities.

## Falsifiable next assay proposal, not executed

First recover the 2019 stimulus/driver methods and register individual receptor locations and deflection directions to named native geoms. Current wall records provide contact IDs, positions and forces, not bristle deflection or an antennal receptor current. Freeze that mapping before event generation. Until then, a TLA perturbation may be called only an imposed ascending-neuron assay.

A bounded neural-only test can ask whether matched unilateral/bilateral pulses in the two exact TLA cells recruit the four exact MDNs, and whether blocking TLA output removes the increment while preserving the imposed TLA spikes. Prespecify pulse train, baseline, latency/count windows, seeds and no-input/readout controls; compare a held-out pulse duration without changing Shiu gains. Failure to obtain the specified response, or physiological failure, must remain a negative result. This tests a structural route, not natural touch transduction.

Physical retreat is a separate unmet requirement: the current body adapter only commands nonnegative forward speed and retains a pretrained policy that has not been validated for backward walking. A successful MDN response cannot presently establish obstacle retreat. Backward kinematics and a supported motor interface must precede that closed-loop claim. No wall coordinates, contact-side turn rule or automatic reference recentering should bypass these missing links.
