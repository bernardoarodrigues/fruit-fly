# Primary evidence for a staged navigation assay

The proposed shift to a causal navigation ladder is useful, but the supplied diagnosis is a hypothesis rather than an established localization. The immediate finite rung should re-score the **existing 60 trials** at exact anatomical stages before choosing one new intervention. No whole-network gain sweep, neural simulation, parameter fit or body experiment was performed for this source audit.

Four primary journal articles and two supplements were read. Their public reading copies, exact hashes, protocol fields and source inconsistencies are retained in [the evidence JSON](../validation/navigation-ladder-assay-evidence.json) and [acquisition receipts](../validation/navigation-ladder-assay-acquisition.json). Full texts are accessible without institutional login. The [supplement receipt](../validation/navigation-ladder-assay-supplement-acquisition.json) covers the genotype tables inspected as rendered PDF pages. This note builds on the existing [navigation calibration](../docs/navigation-calibration.md), [wind boundary](../docs/wind-transduction-boundary.md), and [internal-state notes](../docs/internal-state-learning-plan.md).

## What each assay actually constrains

**Tao, Wechsler and Bhandawat, 2023:** newly eclosed females spent 3–5 days on control food or 4–5 days on retinal food, followed by 15–21 h starvation. A freely walking fly encountered a stationary central optogenetic light zone; trials had 3 min dark baseline and 3 min with the zone illuminated. Or42a alone did not increase time inside in this assay; some ensembles produced stronger but qualitatively different effects. This is not a universal inability of Or42a to affect behavior. Speed, turning and history effects depend on ensemble and locomotor state: **positive odor-ON speed is not a universal target**. The mapping uses firing rate and its change over the preceding 200 ms; longer history is time since first entry. Six replayed 60 s light histories and single-sensillum recordings informed an encoder, with 150 ms rate smoothing; the recordings considered ab1A, ab1B and ab2A. These are not per-animal simultaneous recordings of every activated class. Results say a 2 cm diameter light zone; Methods specify 1.25 cm radius. Preserve this discrepancy before reproducing geometry. [Primary paper, Figs. 1–6 and Methods](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-42613-8).

**Matheson et al., 2022:** the relevant Chrimson activation flies were genetically blind norpA males, 3–10 days old, starved about 24 h. Table S1 also labels the Fig. 1 vinegar comparison male; the main text's general statement that other flies were female must not override that panel-specific record. Wind-tunnel trials were 70 s, with a 10 s pulse starting at 30 s, approximately 120 mm/s wind, 1% vinegar or 626 nm light at 26 µW/mm². Broad Orco or Orco+IR8a activation supported upwind movement; tested single classes did not. Broad activation without wind retained OFF search. Analysis used baseline 10–25 s, peripheral ON upwind velocity 0–5 s, ON groundspeed 2–5 s and OFF curvature 0–2 s. Curvature is absolute angular velocity divided by groundspeed. The study excludes samples below 1 mm/s from these kinematic averages and reports movement probability separately. An immobile model's curvature is therefore unavailable, not evidence of zero turning. [Primary paper, Fig. 1 and Methods](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-022-32247-7), [Table S1 and Fig. S1](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41467-022-32247-7/MediaObjects/41467_2022_32247_MOESM1_ESM.pdf).

**The 2024 Matheson addendum** changes the functional cell assignment: VT062617 additionally or predominantly labels hΔK. Original Fig. 5E–I responses and Fig. 6 interventions may therefore arise from hΔK rather than hΔC. Anatomical FB5AB/PFNa inputs to hΔC remain direct, while the corresponding hΔK inputs are indirect. Keep both populations; do not relabel all hΔC anatomy as hΔK or treat the older driver phenotype as type-pure evidence. [Primary addendum](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-46225-8).

**Kathman et al., 2026:** imaging used 7–12-day-old females starved about 24 h, walking on a ball with closed-loop wind and open-loop 0.5% vinegar. Wind speed was selected per fly from 8, 25 or 33 cm/s during acclimation and sometimes adjusted later. Imaging trials include 15 s odorized wind and 15 s post-odor wind. The **5.59 ± 0.55 s** parameter fits the distribution of post-odor durations of 82 selected bumps from 14 flies, with a 95% confidence interval from curve fitting; it is not a fluorescence decay, synaptic time constant or individual-neuron lifetime. Bumps use ΔF/F thresholding, minimum duration 3 s, and merging of gaps shorter than 3 s. The separate freely walking assay used 3–7-day females, 18–24 h starvation, approximately 120 mm/s wind and 500 ms pulses of 1% vinegar at 1 Hz. Peak upwind probability is measured 2–6 s after first onset; persistence is the first 10 s after final offset. [Primary final paper, Figs. 1 and 5, Methods; published July 25, version of record August 27](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-75945-2).

Kathman's specific hΔK line is **SS63158**, VT062617-AD/VT037223-DB. Its post-odor silencing contrast is significant after ten pulses, not after one or five under the reported correction. Supplementary Table 2 gives 23/24/24 experimental flies and 26/28/29 controls; the main Fig. 5g caption repeats the control sample sizes. Use the separated table cohorts, pending source-data confirmation. Supplementary Fig. 1a supports increased upwind velocity and straightness during odor, but not an overall forward-speed increase. Thus useful persistence should be assessed with spatially localized activity, goal-related orientation and behavior together; diffuse spikes are neither its equivalent nor proof that all persistence is pathological. The paper's optimal 6.4 s value belongs to its particular behavioral model and plume. [Supplement, Figs. S1/S4 and Tables 1–2](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41467-026-75945-2/MediaObjects/41467_2026_75945_MOESM1_ESM.pdf).

## Finite first rung and later assay targets

**First rung: one saved-data stage audit.** Use the already frozen four arms × five conditions × three seeds, with their seven original half-open windows: 0–0.05, 0.05–0.5, 0.5–1, 1–1.5, 1.5–2, 2–2.5 and 2.5–3 s. Pin the exact stage selections from the anatomical audit, keeping exact types, broader families, hΔC/hΔK and full descending-neuron superclass distinct. Recompute per-cell/cohort counts, active fractions, distributions and matched pulse contrasts; report off-bin persistence relative to each trial's recovery and matched constant control. Require complete windows and nonempty, identified cohorts before computing rates. An absent cohort is missing; a populated cohort with no spikes is a complete zero. Do not infer millisecond latency, PN synaptic depression, FB bump position or voltage from count-only checkpoints. Any such measurement requires the corresponding retained spike/state/column mapping evidence.

This rung answers whether recruitment and contrast reach the named populations **under the existing narrow imposed input**. It does not test motion, wind direction, hunger, source seeking or navigation memory. Success means trustworthy operands and a finite list of candidate boundaries, not positive firing everywhere or H1 selection. If an exact immediate PN population is silent despite actual source firing, that is a candidate transfer boundary, not proof of a unique faulty edge. If it is active, follow its measured contrast into the next exact stage. Complete this single audit, then choose **one** prospective intervention with its source, waveform, schedule, control and readout frozen; do not automatically launch a clamp at every stage.

The first later behavioral target can be a **still-air optogenetic-like onset/offset assay**, avoiding an uncalibrated wind-direction claim. A 10 s broad-stimulus exposure with at least 2 s of post-offset observation matches Matheson's OFF-search window; use the named broad expression boundary only after a defensible crosswalk, or call a fixed annotated ensemble an engineering substitute. Retain no-stimulus and same-input outgoing-suppression controls, movement probability, signed angular velocity, speed and curvature with its valid denominator. A short open-loop pulse is not Tao's spatially contingent six-minute protocol or a test of its long history effects. No numeric broad-input rate is assigned here, because none was recovered from these protocols.

For the separate wind rung, the compact behavioral comparisons are odor only, wind only, both and neither; left/right reflected wind adds a directional test. A fed/hungry comparison should be added only as a specified state manipulation, not inferred from labels. Kathman's pulse-number and post-offset orientation assay is a later memory target: it requires enough observation time, a declared heading reference, spatial neural readouts and an explicit observation model if comparing simulated spikes with fluorescence. It cannot be scored on the existing 3 s trials.

## Corrections to the supplied causal account

- **H1 was not an inhibitory-gain weakening experiment.** In the completed factorial, graph gains stayed fixed. H0/H1 changed the inhibitory equation; C1/H1 changed the bundled decay/refractory-reception/reset-retention handling. Older gain screens are separate evidence. Bounded negative voltage alone did not restore the chosen motor outputs, but this does not prove inhibition irrelevant to navigation.
- The proposed route is a useful measurement map, not one obligatory chain. Tao explicitly discusses parallel PN/higher-order paths; Matheson found that silencing individual navigation-promoting MB/LH lines did not abolish upwind movement. Structural contact totals establish neither unique routing nor physiological transmission. [Tao](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-42613-8), [Matheson](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-022-32247-7).
- A downstream clamp that produces the intended output establishes **conditional sufficiency of that imposed activity and decoder**. It may bypass several defects, recruit parallel routes, overwhelm recurrent inhibition or create a non-natural pattern. It does not uniquely identify the first broken boundary. Necessity/suppression, matched waveform replay and intervening recordings are distinct follow-up contrasts, not conclusions from rescue alone.
- Direct DNg97 stimulation plus motor-readout muting supports the tested actuator route. It does not validate the complete decoder, all kinematic responses or the claim that the principal biological failure has been localized. The current evidence permits multiple coupled failures. Use the ladder to discriminate them.

The actionable derived insight is methodological: choose an observable that the preparation measures, and keep anatomical reachability, neural transfer, downstream sufficiency and natural behavior as separate claims. No new biological finding is claimed.
